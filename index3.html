Can I Golf Here?

Type a city or ZIP to see an area's golfability score based on weather conditions.

Scoring (v3):
  • Starts at 100 and subtracts points for adverse weather conditions
  • Score 71–100: bombs away
  • Score 40–70: gear up and get out there
  • Score 26–39: sickos only
  • Score 0–25: simulator will have to do
  • Any standing snow (measured or inferred): score is forced to 0
This is weather-only. Courses can still be closed for other reasons.
